Origin Story

Four Tables.
One Family Recipe.

Mamma Mia began not with a business plan, but with a question: what would it feel like to open your home to strangers and cook for them the way you cook for the people you love?

In 2018, husband-and-wife duo Rizal and Nadia Santoso converted a small rented space in the heart of Semarang into a four-table warung. No investors, no press, no Instagram strategy — just Nadia's grandmother's sambal recipe and Rizal's instinct for hospitality.

Word spread slowly at first, then all at once. By the end of their first year, the waitlist for weekend dinners stretched around the block. Mamma Mia had become something people talked about not because of what was on the plate, but because of how eating there made them feel.

The original space, 2018

Our Philosophy

Italian Soul,
Indonesian Heart.

Mamma Mia draws its culinary identity from Italian tradition — the belief that great food starts with the finest ingredients, the least interference, and the most patience. But the flavors that fill our kitchen are deeply rooted in the Indonesian pantry: keluak, rempah, pandan, kelapa, and a dozen other ingredients that hold the memory of this country in every bite.

We call this approach cucina onesta — honest cooking. No shortcuts, no stock powder, no compromise. Every broth is made from scratch. Every sambal is ground fresh. Every pastry is made in-house before the sun rises.

I

Ingredients First

We source directly from farmers and suppliers we trust personally. If the ingredient isn't right, the dish doesn't go on the menu.

II

Respect the Process

Slow cooking, proper technique, and patience. We believe every great dish is earned, not rushed.

III

Feed with Intention

Every plate that leaves our kitchen carries care. We want every guest to feel that — not just taste it.

The People Behind Mamma Mia

Our Founders

Rizal Santoso

Co-Founder & Director of Operations

Rizal grew up watching his mother run a small catering business out of their family home in Semarang. That upbringing gave him an intuitive grasp of hospitality — the quiet art of making people feel welcome before they've even taken their seats.

After a decade in hotel management across Jakarta and Bali, he returned to Semarang with a clear vision: a restaurant that felt personal. Not a concept, not a brand — a place. Mamma Mia is that place.

"A restaurant is only as good as the feeling people carry out the door."

Nadia Santoso

Co-Founder & Head of Culinary

Nadia trained at a culinary institute in Bologna, Italy, where she spent three years immersed in the philosophy of Italian cooking — its precision, its seasonality, its refusal to overcomplicate. She came home with techniques, but kept her grandmother's recipes.

Her cooking is the soul of Mamma Mia. She writes every menu, oversees every recipe, and still personally trains each kitchen hire. The consistency you taste at Mamma Mia is entirely hers.

"Italian cooking taught me restraint. Indonesian ingredients taught me depth. Mamma Mia is where those two things finally made sense together."
