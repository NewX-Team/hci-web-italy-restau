Our Story

Born From a Small Kitchen in Semarang

Marca started in 2018 as a tiny warung with four tables, a handful of family recipes, and a stubborn belief that good food doesn't need to be complicated — just honest.

What began as a weekend passion project grew, plate by plate, into the place you're visiting today. Every dish still carries that same original intention: feed people well, make them feel welcome.
