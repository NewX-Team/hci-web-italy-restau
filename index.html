Our Story

Inspired by an Ancient Kitchen in Florence

Mamma Mia was born from a deep love for Italian culinary tradition. Starting as a small trattoria in the heart of Florence in 2018, we bring family recipes passed down through generations to your table.

Every ingredient is hand-selected from local farmers in Tuscany and Piedmont. From cold-pressed extra virgin olive oil to perfectly aged Parmigiano Reggiano. We don't just serve food; we present a story of Italy on every plate.
